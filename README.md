# Oliver-s-Why-Models-Work

Research notes and technical insights on:

- Efficient Vision Models
- Mamba Architectures
- Vehicle Re-Identification
- Multi-modal Retrieval
- Edge AI Systems
- Embedded Intelligence

Focused on understanding how modern AI systems
learn, optimize, and operate under real-world constraints.

## Philosophy

This repository is not a collection of paper summaries.
The goal is to analyze:

- why methods work
- how information flows through models
- what optimization behaviors emerge
- what engineering trade-offs exist
- how ideas transfer across domains

The focus is long-term research understanding
instead of short-term paper digestion.
